============================================================

WHAT THIS CONTENT MEANS:
This report analyzes how the stock market actually "moves" during major events. 
1. TURNOVER (Money): Measures the total financial weight of trading.
2. TRADES (Density): Measures the number of individual human/system actions.
3. VOLATILITY (Fear): Measures the price gap (High-Low) showing market nervousness.

WHY THIS IS PROVIDED:
To satisfy the internship objective of linking "Company Situations" to "Market Behavior."
It proves that events like Mergers or Profit Drops aren't just news—they are mathematical 
triggers that change liquidity, panic levels, and exchange preference (NSE vs BSE).
